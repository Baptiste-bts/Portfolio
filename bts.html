DIPLOMA OVERVIEW

The BTS SIO, in short.

The BTS SIO (IT Services for Organizations) trains technicians to deploy, secure and maintain organizations' information systems. It's a two-year program with two possible tracks.

TWO TRACKS

SISR and SLAM

Both tracks share a common core (general culture, English, economics & law, management) before specializing.

SISR — my track

Infrastructure, Systems and Network Solutions. Server administration, networking, virtualization, security and information system monitoring.

SLAM

Software and Business Application Solutions. Designing, developing and maintaining applications and databases for organizations.

IN THE SISR CURRICULUM

What my training covers

System administration (Windows / Linux) Networking & telecoms Virtualization & cloud Cybersecurity Databases (MySQL) Web development (HTML, CSS, JS) Python Project management User support & assistance

MY SCHOOL

Lycée Pierre Poivre

I'm completing my BTS SIO, SISR track, at Lycée Pierre Poivre in Saint-Joseph, Réunion Island, since 2025. The program combines coursework, hands-on labs in a network lab, and projects carried out collaboratively, under conditions similar to a real IT department.
