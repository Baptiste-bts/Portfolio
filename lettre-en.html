PROFESSIONAL WRITING EXERCISE

Cover letter

Cover letter written as part of my second year of BTS SIO, SISR track, as an example of a professional application. To be personalized with the company and recipient's name before each submission.

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am currently in my second year of the BTS Information Technology Services for Organizations program, Infrastructure, Systems and Network Solutions track, at Lycée Pierre Poivre in Saint-Joseph. I am looking for an internship to put my technical skills into practice within an IT team.

My training has given me a solid foundation in system administration (Windows, Linux), networking, relational databases (MySQL), as well as web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) and Python scripting. I was able to apply these skills during an IT support internship at the Le Port public library, where I handled the installation and maintenance of workstations, user assistance and network incident follow-up, while also leading introductory programming sessions for middle school students.

My time in Germany as part of the Erasmus program, in Heidenheim, also helped me develop my teamwork skills, adaptability and ease working in a multicultural environment — qualities I consider essential for working within an IT department.

Rigorous, curious and comfortable both solving technical problems and interacting with users, I would now like to join your company to keep learning in the field and contribute concretely to your projects.

I remain available for an interview and look forward to hearing from you.

Baptiste Bousser

Tip: keep this text as a template and adjust the 2nd paragraph depending on the position (support, networking, system administration…) and the 3rd paragraph depending on what you know about the company.
